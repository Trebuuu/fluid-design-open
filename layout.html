Layout

One column. Twelve tracks.

Every product page hangs off one centred column: 87.5% of the window, never wider than 1260 pixels. Twelve tracks divide it, twenty pixels apart. Bands stack down the page in a fixed rhythm, and every corner is one of three kinds.

Bands

Story, utility, stage.

A page is a stack of full-width bands. White story bands carry the argument, grey utility bands carry the details, black stage bands carry material and film. The frame is a real page at 1440 pixels, measured as it renders.

    Radii

    Twenty-eight is the radius.

    Cards, galleries and modals all use 28. Everything else is a step either side of it. Each is drawn as a continuous corner, so the number you write is the nominal radius and the curve is a continuous one.

    A corner that eases in.

    A circular corner bends all at once. A continuous corner eases into the straight edge, so it starts earlier and cuts in no deeper. CSS draws it with corner-shape: superellipse(1.71) and the radius scaled by 1.53 — the same curve within a fraction of a pixel. Browsers without corner-shape get the circle of equal mass.

    Concentric

    Inside radius, derived.

    A shape inside a shape shares its centre: inner radius = outer radius − the gap, never below 6. Mark the child data-concentric and the gap is measured, not assumed — drag the slider and watch it re-derive.
