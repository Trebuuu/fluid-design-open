Three sources, one system

Measured, not asserted.

Fluid Design Open takes a measured set of web tokens, the glass material as a stack of measured layers, and a saturated, art-led look built on one set of engines. Every number on this page was read from a rendered page, not typed from memory.

Measured tokens

The type ramp, band rhythm, radii, springs and scroll keyframes, read from computed styles at 1440 and 402 px.

The glass material

The material as a stack: a lensed rim, a colour transform, a top highlight, a dark edge and an adaptive shadow. Shapes morph by identity.

One look, one set of engines

Saturated art, dusty primary and strong lens, running on superellipse geometry, a spring solver and an OKLCH palette engine.

  2. Computed styles of nine roles (hero, section, lede, eyebrow, subtitle, body, micro, stat and stat caption) compared with their reference values at 1440 px in Edge 153: size, line height and tracking all equal. At 402 px the hero follows the lockup step, 40/44, where a single marquee headline is 48/52; the hero on this page uses 48.
  3. Diagonal inset of the fitted corner (superellipse k 1.71, radius × 1.53) against a reconstruction of the continuous corner, solved analytically for r12, r18, r28 and r44. Rendered in Edge 153 the insets land within 0.5 px. Where a browser has no corner-shape, FG3 draws the exact clip or a circle of equal mass.
  4. Displacement map from the engine’s own builder: a 720 × 50 capsule, bezel 17, the default configuration. Refraction runs in Chromium on a hardware GPU; other browsers draw the same glass as frost.
  5. Largest gap, as a share of travel, between each emitted CSS linear() easing and the analytic spring it came from, across all 24 named springs.
  6. Mean main-thread time per frame of a pill-to-panel morph in headless Edge with a GPU, 0.52 to 0.97 ms; frames that bake a new corner tile take 5 to 11 ms. Before the tile cache it was 17.4 ms.
  7. OKLCH mean chroma of the raw hero canvas; vivid means chroma ≥ .08. The gate is checked on the rendered hero crop.
