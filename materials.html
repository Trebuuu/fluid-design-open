Anatomy

Six layers. One surface.

Glass is drawn as a stack of layers, and LG3 does the same. Switch any layer off and both the assembled glass and its exploded twin lose it.

Any ancestor with opacity below 1, a filter, a mask or a blend mode cuts the glass off from what is behind it. Nothing throws; the lens just goes flat. Animate transforms on ancestors, and use LG3.materialize / LG3.dematerialize on the glass.

Tint 0.50

Appearance › Glass

  • ClearTinted

Applies to every glass surface on this page, the navigation included. The slider is off while Reduce Transparency or Increase Contrast is on.

The midpoint is exactly the measured recipe. Toward clear, the wash and blur thin; toward tinted, they thicken until the glass is nearly a solid.

The slider has no published end points. LG3’s curve is its own, anchored so 0.5 is the default recipe.

What Safari and Firefox get

WebKit parses backdrop-filter: url() and then drops the whole backdrop, so asking CSS.supports lies. LG3 refracts only on Chromium with a hardware GPU. Everywhere else, and on software renderers, glass takes the frost path: the same blur, the same colour transform, highlight, dark edge and shadow — no bending at the rim.

A 50 px capsule bends the backdrop by at least 8 px at the rim and 2 px on average (acceptance). Measured here at load.
