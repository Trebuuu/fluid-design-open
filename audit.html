Audit

Every page, measured as it paints.

Each page loads here for real and is walked twice. Every line of text is measured against the ground it actually sits on — the art canvas’s own pixels, a gradient at that point, or the glass and whatever it is bending — and every control is checked for a keyboard path and a visible ring.

Run the audit to walk every page in the manifest. It takes a few seconds a page.

How it measures.

The ground is the paint stack.

At several points inside every text box the audit asks the browser what is painted there, top to bottom, and composites it: background colours, linear, radial and conic gradients evaluated at that point, positioned overlays, SVG fills, and the ART3 canvas’s own pixels with its mask and opacity. The worst point is the result.

Glass is measured, not skipped.

Under a glass host the ground is sampled across the blur radius, averaged, and passed through that host’s own backdrop-filter chain — or, for the refraction path, through LG3.material()’s measured colour transform. Refraction moves pixels only at the rim, so it is left out for text inside the host.

What is not counted as a pass.

  • Unmeasurable: an image or video under the text, glass on glass, a transformed pseudo-element, a canvas that cannot be read. Each is listed with its reason.
  • Exempt: inactive controls (WCAG names them) and anything marked data-specimen, which shows a colour rather than speaking in it.
  • Thresholds: 4.5:1, or 3:1 for large text — 24 px, or 19 px at semibold (SPEC’s rule; WCAG’s is 18.66 px bold).
  • At rest: pages load in the base experience, so every reveal sits in its final state — what a reader with reduced motion sees.

Checked against the screen.

Driven headless, the audit prints one row per page and exits 1 on any failure. With --verify every failing box, and a sample of passes on art and glass, is screenshotted and measured again from its pixels. Where the two disagree it says so — small type inside a scaled device screen reads low in pixels, and a box half under an overlay differs.

node tools/run-audit.mjs
node tools/run-audit.mjs --width 402
node tools/run-audit.mjs --verify --pages index.html,color.html
