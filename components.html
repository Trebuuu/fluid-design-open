Glass controls.

Glass is for controls that float over something: art, media, scrolling content. Press one and it lifts, glows from the touch point and bends what is behind it. Open the round button: it becomes the menu.

  • Only over something to bend.

    Glass sits on art, media or scrolling content. On a flat ground it is a bordered grey box; use an opaque control.

  • One tinted primary per view.

    Tint the glass body, never the label. Every other glass control stays colourless and monochrome.

  • Never glass on glass.

    A menu that opens from glass replaces it by morphing; it never stacks a second lens on the first.

The UI script.

Every component above renders from CSS alone. ui.js adds only what the platform does not: anchoring, entrances that grow from their trigger, the sliding indicator, steppers that repeat, toasts and alerts. FG3.boot() mounts it; you never call UI.mount yourself unless you add markup later.

CallReturnsWhat it does
UI.mount(root)number wiredWires segmented, steppers, sliders, detents, [data-tip], [data-menu], [data-popover], card hover and rings under root. Idempotent.
UI.anchor(panel, trigger, opts){placement, path, rect}CSS anchor positioning where supported, a measured fixed placement elsewhere. placement, align, gap.
UI.menu(trigger, panel, opts){open, close, toggle, isOpen, destroy}Glass panel + glass trigger with one data-lg3-id morph; other glass materializes; opaque springs. Emits ui:select.
UI.popover(trigger, panel, opts)same as menuFree content anchored to its trigger; focus moves into it.
UI.toast(message, opts){el, dismiss}kind ok, bad, info; action {label, run}; duration; glass. A polite live region.
UI.alert(opts)Promise<value>title, message, actions [{label, value, role}], from. Esc resolves the cancel value.
UI.tooltip(el, text){show, hide}420 ms in, instant out, on focus too.
UI.tooltips(root)number wiredWires every [data-tip] under root; UI.mount calls it.
UI.segmented(host, opts){index, select, place, destroy}Roving tabindex, arrows, Home, End, drag to scrub. Emits change {index, value, item}.
UI.stepper(el){value, set}Repeats while held; arrows, Home, End. Emits change {value}.
UI.slider(input){paint}Writes --v, the filled part of the track.

Rules the script keeps

Never self-mounts
FG3.boot() calls UI.mount(document)
Degrades
Without LG3, MOTION or SYM every part still works, flatter
Interruptible
Every entrance is a spring that can be reversed mid-flight
Reduced motion
Displacement goes; state still lands
Light dismiss
Menus use popover="manual" so the exit can animate
